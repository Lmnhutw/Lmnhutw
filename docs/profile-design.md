STORY: Meet Vincent, open Narrative Studio, explore five projects and grouped inventory, inspect real GitHub activity, finish at a moonlit cat dialogue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact terminal header, three-column room/identity/sidebar Hero and full-width illustrated Current Quest. Quest Log and Inventory align in two equal columns below. Preserve name/project emphasis without billboard typography.

FORM: User-selected reference composition; no concept lottery or alternative identity. Mobile gets art-directed SVG variants while the project/inventory pair stays in two columns.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
